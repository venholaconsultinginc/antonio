# Namesake

This repository is named for **Antonio**, the sea captain in Shakespeare's *Twelfth Night*.

Early in the play he rescues Sebastian from a shipwreck, then takes a real risk on his behalf.
Illyria isn't safe for him; he has enemies there from an old skirmish. He follows Sebastian into
the city anyway, hands over his own money, and tells him to go enjoy the town and find his own way.
He doesn't try to manage what Sebastian does with it, and he doesn't tag along to supervise. He
makes sure Sebastian is equipped to get started, then steps back.

That's the relationship this repo is meant to have with the people who use it. Enough working
example code, sample data and documentation to get you going quickly, and no more than that. It
isn't a framework you depend on or a tool you keep coming back to. Copy what's useful, adapt it,
and go make it your own, same as Sebastian does.

*(Antonio doesn't get an easy ending for his trouble. He's arrested by Illyrian officers partway
through, and the play resolves around Sebastian and Viola rather than him. The metaphor is about
the help he gives at the start, not a promise about how things turn out.)*
